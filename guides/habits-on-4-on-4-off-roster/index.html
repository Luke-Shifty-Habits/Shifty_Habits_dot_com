How to keep habits going on a 4-on, 4-off roster

Four twelve-hour shifts, four days off, repeat. It's one of the most common rosters there is, and one of the hardest to build habits on with a normal habit app — because eight days never line up with a seven-day week.

By Luke — shift worker, and the developer of Shifty Habits

The short answer: treat the eight-day cycle as your week. Give your four work days only small, minimum versions of your habits — twelve-hour shifts don't leave room for more — and give each of your four days off a job: recover, live, live, reset. Then judge your habits per cycle, not per calendar week.

What the cycle looks like

The simplest version is four day shifts and four days off, repeating every eight days:

DDDD––––

Many employers alternate day and night blocks, giving a sixteen-day cycle:

DDDD–––– NNNN––––

And some split each block into two days and two nights (D D N N – – – –). The advice below works for all three; the rotation patterns guide compares 4-on-4-off with the other common rosters.

Why habits break on 4-on-4-off

Eight doesn't divide into seven. Your days off shift one weekday later every cycle, so a habit you planned for "weekends" or "Tuesdays and Thursdays" lands on a work day most of the time. Weekday-based habit apps then mark it missed. After a few cycles of that, most people give up on the app, the habit, or both.

The other problem is the shape: four long days with nearly nothing left over, then four days with all the time in the world. A routine designed for an average day fits neither.

Work days: minimum versions only

A twelve-hour shift plus a commute plus sleep leaves an hour or two at most. Don't plan anything big for work days. Plan the smallest version of each habit that keeps it alive:

  • A ten-minute walk, not a workout
  • Five pages, not a chapter
  • Packed food, not cooking
  • In bed on time — on a 4-on-4-off, this is the habit everything else depends on

Give each day off a job

Day offJobHabits that belong here
1RecoverSleep, food, daylight, an easy walk — especially after nights
2LiveProper training session, people, personal goals
3LiveSecond training session, the bigger projects
4ResetBatch cook, laundry, kit ready, shift your sleep towards the next block

Giving days off a job stops them dissolving into one long blur — and the "reset" day means the next work block starts prepared rather than from scratch. If the next block is nights, use day four to let your bedtime drift later.

Judge your habits per cycle

Measure in eights, not sevens. "Four training sessions per cycle" is a goal you can actually hit every time; "three a week" will be easy some weeks and impossible in others, depending on where your days off fall. Look back at the end of each cycle, adjust, and give any new habit at least two or three full cycles — about a month — before you judge it.

Frequently asked questions

How can I keep habits going on a 4-on, 4-off roster?

Treat the eight-day cycle as your week. Keep only small, minimum versions of habits on your four work days, give each of your four days off a job — recover, live, live, reset — and judge your habits per cycle rather than per calendar week, since your days off move through the week every cycle.

How long is a 4-on-4-off roster cycle?

Eight days if every block is the same shift type: four shifts followed by four days off. Many employers alternate a block of days with a block of nights, which makes the full cycle sixteen days. Either way it doesn't line up with the seven-day week, so your days off move one weekday later every cycle.

What should I do with my four days off?

Give each a job. Use the first to recover, especially after nights; the second and third for training, people and personal goals; and the fourth to reset for the next block with batch cooking, laundry and adjusting your sleep.

Can a habit app follow a 4-on-4-off roster?

Most general habit apps can't, because they schedule habits by weekday and eight days never line up with a seven-day week. Shifty Habits lets you build the 4-on-4-off cycle once and attach habits to day shifts, night shifts or days off, so they follow the roster automatically.
