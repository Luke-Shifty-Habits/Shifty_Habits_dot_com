Guides for shift workers

Practical writing about building habits and routines around a roster — from someone who works one. No generic habit advice that assumes you're up at seven every morning.

Anchor habits to shift events instead of clock times, give each one a version per shift type, and count opportunities taken rather than calendar days.

Protecting the sleep window, using light in both directions, caffeine timing, eating on nights, and surviving the changeover days at either end of a block.

Common shift rotation patterns explained

4-on-4-off, 2-2-3 Panama, DuPont, Continental and Southern Swing — drawn out day by day, with what each one does to your sleep and your weekends.

Why your habit streak keeps breaking, and how to stop it

What to look for in a habit tracker for shift workers

The checklist for choosing a habit tracker that works on a rotating roster or an irregular schedule.

How to build a routine without a fixed wake-up time

Routines that hang off waking and sleeping instead of the clock, including a morning routine for when you work nights.

How to get back into a routine after night shift — and keep it on days off

A plan for the first day after a run of nights, and for keeping a routine alive across your days off.

How to manage your sleep routine when working shifts

Plan sleep per shift type, protect the sleep window, and keep the same wind-down whatever time you go to bed.

How to exercise consistently when you work rotating shifts

Pick a slot for each shift type, plan training by the roster rather than the week, and keep a minimum version for nights.

How to eat healthy when you work irregular hours

Plan meals by shift type, eat your main meal before nights, keep overnight food light, and prep on your last day off.
