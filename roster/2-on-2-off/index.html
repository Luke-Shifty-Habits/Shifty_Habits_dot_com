2-on-2-off shift calendar

Two shifts, two days off, repeat. Set the day your cycle starts and see every shift for the year.

The short answer: A 2-on-2-off roster is a four-day cycle: two shifts, then two days off. With 12-hour shifts it averages 42 hours a week, and your days off move through the week, falling on the same weekdays only every four weeks.

The 2-on-2-off pattern

One full cycle, one square per day: D day shift, N night shift, – day off.

DD––
Cycle length4 days
Shift lengthusually 12 hours
Average week42 hours with 12-hour shifts

It's a short, even rotation: you never work more than two days in a row, but you're never off for more than two either.

The Panama (2-2-3) pattern is a close relative that adds three-day blocks so the cycle lines up with a fortnight; see its calendar.

Frequently asked questions

How does a 2-on-2-off roster work?

You work two shifts, then have two days off, and the four-day cycle repeats. With 12-hour shifts that averages 42 hours a week.

Do 2-on-2-off days off fall on the same days each week?

No. A four-day cycle moves through the week, so the same days off only come round on the same weekdays every four weeks.

How do I see my 2-on-2-off roster on a calendar?

Open the calendar on this page, set the date of your first shift as day 1, and it shows every shift and day off for the next twelve months. You can share it as a link or print it.
