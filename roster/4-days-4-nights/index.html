4 days, 4 nights, 4 off: shift calendar

Four days, four off, four nights, four off: the days-and-nights version of 4-on-4-off. Set when your cycle starts and see the whole year.

The short answer: This roster is a 16-day cycle: four 12-hour day shifts, four days off, four 12-hour night shifts and four more days off. It averages 42 hours a week, and like every 4-on-4-off pattern it drifts through the week.

The calendar needs JavaScript turned on. The pattern is described below.

The 4 days, 4 nights pattern

One full cycle, one square per day: D day shift, N night shift, – day off.

DDDD––––NNNN––––
Cycle length16 days
Shift length12 hours
Average week42 hours

It's the most common way 24/7 sites run 4-on-4-off: each crew takes a block of days, then a block of nights, with four days between them to turn the body clock around.

The four days off after nights do two jobs: the first usually goes on sleep, and the last is where you shift your bedtime back for the next block of days.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a 4 days, 4 nights, 4 off cycle?

Sixteen days: four day shifts, four days off, four night shifts and four days off. With 12-hour shifts it averages 42 hours a week.

Is 4 days 4 nights the same as 4-on-4-off?

It's the most common form of it. Plain 4-on-4-off can mean the same shift every block, an eight-day cycle; when the blocks alternate between days and nights the full cycle is sixteen days.

How do I see my 4 days, 4 nights roster on a calendar?

Open the calendar on this page, set the date of your first day shift as day 1, and change the shift times to yours. It shows every day shift, night shift and day off for the next twelve months, and you can share it as a link.
