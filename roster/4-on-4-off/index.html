4-on-4-off shift calendar

Four shifts, four days off, repeat. Set the day your cycle starts and see exactly where every shift and every day off lands for the next year.

The short answer: A 4-on-4-off roster is an eight-day cycle: four 12-hour shifts, then four days off. It averages 42 hours a week, and because eight days doesn't divide into a week, your days off move one weekday later every cycle.

The 4-on-4-off pattern

One full cycle, one square per day: D day shift, N night shift, – day off.

Many employers alternate blocks, so you work four days, have four off, then four nights and four off. That makes a 16-day cycle; it has its own calendar.

The drift is what makes this pattern hard to plan around: your days off only land on the same weekdays again every eight weeks. The calendar below shows exactly where they fall.

Frequently asked questions

How does a 4-on-4-off roster work?

You work four shifts, usually 12 hours each, then have four days off, and the eight-day cycle repeats. Over time that averages 42 hours a week.

Do 4-on-4-off days off fall on the same days each week?

No. Eight days doesn't divide into a seven-day week, so each cycle starts one weekday later than the last, and your days off only fall on the same weekdays again every eight weeks.

How do I see my 4-on-4-off roster on a calendar?
