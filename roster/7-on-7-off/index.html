7-on-7-off shift calendar

A week on, a week off. Set the day your working week starts and see every week on and week off for the year.

The short answer: A 7-on-7-off roster is a 14-day cycle: seven shifts in a row, then seven days off. With 12-hour shifts it averages 42 hours a week, and because it is exactly two weeks it starts on the same weekday every time.

The calendar needs JavaScript turned on. The pattern is described below.

The 7-on-7-off pattern

One full cycle, one square per day: D day shift, N night shift, – day off.

DDDDDDD–––––––
Cycle length14 days
Shift lengthusually 12 hours
Average week42 hours with 12-hour shifts

Seven 12-hour shifts in a row is demanding, so the week on is mostly work and sleep; the week off is where everything else happens.

Frequently asked questions

How does a 7-on-7-off roster work?

You work seven shifts in a row, then have seven days off, and the fortnight repeats. With 12-hour shifts that averages 42 hours a week.

Does a 7-on-7-off roster start on the same day each time?

Yes. The cycle is exactly 14 days, so every working week starts on the same weekday, a fortnight apart.

How do I see my 7-on-7-off roster on a calendar?

Open the calendar on this page, set the first day of a working week as day 1, and it shows every week on and week off for the next twelve months. You can share it as a link or print it.
