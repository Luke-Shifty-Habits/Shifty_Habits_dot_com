DuPont shift calendar

Four nights, three off, three days, one off, three nights, three off, four days, then seven days off. See where every block lands for the year.

The short answer: The DuPont schedule is a 28-day cycle of 12-hour shifts: four nights, three off, three days, one off, three nights, three off, four days, then seven days off. It averages 42 hours a week across four crews.

The calendar needs JavaScript turned on. The pattern is described below.

The DuPont pattern

One full cycle, one square per day: D day shift, N night shift, – day off.

NNNN–––DDD–NNN–––DDDD–––––––
Cycle length28 days
Shift length12 hours
Average week42 hours

The seven-day break every four weeks is the whole appeal: a week off without touching your leave. The single day off between a night block and a day block is the hardest part of the cycle.

Because 28 days is exactly four weeks, DuPont repeats on the same weekdays every cycle, so your long break lands on the same days each month.

Frequently asked questions

What is the DuPont shift schedule?

A 28-day rotation of 12-hour shifts worked by four crews: four nights, three off, three days, one off, three nights, three off, four days, then seven consecutive days off. It averages 42 hours a week.

How often do you get a week off on DuPont?

Every 28 days. The cycle ends with seven consecutive days off, and because 28 days is exactly four weeks it falls on the same weekdays every cycle.

How do I see my DuPont roster on a calendar?

Open the calendar on this page, set the date of your first night shift as day 1, and it shows every shift and day off for the next twelve months. Share it as a link or print it.
