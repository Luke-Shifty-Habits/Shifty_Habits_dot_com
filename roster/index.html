    Frequently asked questions

    How do I make a calendar of my shift roster?

    Pick the pattern closest to yours, such as 4-on-4-off, Panama 2-2-3, DuPont or 2 earlies, 2 lates, 2 nights, 4 off, then tap any day of the cycle to change it. Set the date day 1 of your cycle falls on, and the calendar fills in every shift for the next twelve months.

    Is my roster saved or sent anywhere?

    No. The calendar is worked out in your browser. Your roster is kept in the page address after the # sign, which browsers never send to a website, and in your browser's own storage so it is there when you come back. There is no account and nothing is stored on a server.

    How do I share my roster with my partner or family?

    Copy the share link and send it. The link holds the whole roster, so whoever opens it sees the same calendar, on a phone or a computer, without signing up. Anyone with the link can see the roster, so send it only to people you want to have it.

    Can I print my roster?

    Yes. The Print button prints the twelve months in colour, without the rest of the page.

    Can I get my roster into the Shifty Habits app?

    The app's cycle builder has the same patterns, so you can build the same roster there in a minute during setup. The app then ties your habits and reminders to each shift type, so they follow the roster automatically. It is free on Google Play for Android.
