Panama shift calendar (2-2-3)

Two on, two off, three on, then the mirror image. Set the day your fortnight starts and see every shift and every long weekend for the year.

The short answer: The Panama pattern, also called 2-2-3 or Pitman, is a 14-day cycle of 12-hour shifts: two on, two off, three on, two off, two on, three off. That's 84 hours a fortnight, 42 a week on average, on the same weekdays every fortnight.

The Panama (2-2-3) pattern

One full cycle, one square per day: D day shift, N night shift, – day off.

DD––DDD––DD–––
Cycle length14 days
Shift length12 hours
Average week42 hours

Because the cycle is exactly two weeks, it doesn't drift: every other weekend is a three-day break, and the heavy weekend you work comes round on schedule.

Many sites alternate a Panama fortnight on days with one on nights, a 28-day cycle. Pick the pattern, then add the nights fortnight in the cycle builder.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Panama shift pattern?

A 14-day cycle of 12-hour shifts: two on, two off, three on, two off, two on, three off. It works out at seven shifts a fortnight, 84 hours, or 42 a week on average. It is also called 2-2-3 or Pitman.

Does a Panama roster fall on the same days every fortnight?

Yes. The cycle is exactly 14 days, so it repeats on the same weekdays every two weeks, with every other weekend off as a three-day break.

How do I see my Panama roster on a calendar?

Open the calendar on this page and set the date your fortnight starts as day 1. It shows every shift and day off for the next twelve months, and you can share it as a link or print it.
